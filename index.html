About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, where I work with Prof. Mohammad Hajiesmaili. In summer 2026, I was a visiting researcher at MIT Sloan School of Management, working with Prof. Thodoris Lykouris.

My research focuses on online learning and bandit algorithms robust to adversarial corruption, including multi-agent learning, combinatorial bandits, and learning to rank.
